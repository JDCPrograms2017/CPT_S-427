## Project

DocuSprite is a project that I started in my internship as a Software  
Engineer Intern which is geared towards eliminating the waste of engineering  
hours billed to the tedious task of requirements management across the company.  

Our internal requirements management system is used widely across the company to  
manage the requiremements that the company utilizes to specify the way that our  
products are built, maintained, preserved, improved upon, and other such  
necessities. The issue is that these standards usually come from PDF or DOCX  
sources which isn't natively compatible with our system. The internal requirements  
management software uses a proprietary block-based document structure that  
engineers spend countless hours on just copying information over from documents.  

This is where DocuSprite comes in. DocuSprite is an automated OCR (optical  
character recognition) pipeline that uses efficient AI models to identify, extract,  
and upload information from PDF and DOCX documents to Selarium in the block-based  
format that the program expects.
